# Construct v1.3.0

Worlds: whole constructs placed together in one scene, made in Hermes from the chat. Your chair, clock and synthesizer in
your house, your cars under it, a ship overhead, a planet in the sky; a street, a city, a game map. The full list is
in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md), and the format in [Worlds](../WORLDS.md).

## A world keeps each construct whole

A world is not a bigger construct. Each placed construct keeps its parts, sources, provenance, critic's verdicts and
joints under its placement's id (`vespa/legshield`). A world has budgets of its own, places other worlds eight levels
deep and compiles a construct placed many times once, so a city can hold districts and a district buildings.

## Placed honestly

Things stand on the part they rest on (`on: villa/living-floor-east`) at real size. A planet goes in the sky at its
real angular size: Saturn as close as the Moon is 17.8° across, its rings twice that. The world's audit says what
overlaps, what runs into the house it stands in, what stands where a door or lid opens, and what floats.

## Everywhere a construct goes

The page draws every joint of a world (they live in a texture now, not sixteen slots) and gives each view a button.
A world exports as one glTF for Blender or as a Godot game map in which every hinged or sliding part moves on its own joint, and the Blender add-on
imports it. Every construct now finishes with its own material map by default (paint, chrome, wood, rubber, glass,
light), which every export uses without being told, and its artwork (signs, labels, screens) comes along as
textures.

## In Hermes

`construct_world` makes a world from the chat and shows each change live: place several constructs at once, move,
remove, list, name views. Constructs come from your projects, the plugin's own (24, from 16), or the gallery by name (a
gallery construct is downloaded from Construct's GitHub release only after you approve it in Hermes's prompt). Six
worlds ship to start from, in the plugin too, the two made from pictures with their constructs and artwork:
- **Rain Harbour**, a game level made in Hermes from one concept picture: 21 constructs placed 50 times, every sign
  in real English and Chinese, a drivable hero car, then 12 rounds of the world against the picture;
- **Stortorget at Christmas**, Stockholm's old square made from one photograph: eight houses researched one by one,
  one market stall placed 41 times, the tree;
- the Villa Savoye lived in, with its furniture, an FJ62 and a DB5 under the pilotis and KESTREL over the roof;
- the shed and the things around it; a street of landmarks; KESTREL at Saturn.

Or arrange it yourself: on a world's card, **Arrange**, then click a construct and drag it; Shift-drag or Q and E
turn it, and what stands on it goes with it. **Add a construct** brings in any construct from the library. **Save**
checks the world with your moves, saves and rebuilds it, with no model turn; **Undo last change** steps back. The
Constructs page has a **Worlds** tab: your worlds and the six to start from.

## From two long runs

Rain Harbour and Stortorget were each about a day of work for the agent, every construct built by its own subagent
with a fresh critic each round. What they ran into became the tool's own: masterpiece rounds that record ties, carry
the picture and the request from round to round, keep each sign's artwork with its checkpoint and can be given more
time (`rounds extend`); checks several times faster on big scenes; big worlds that keep their colours in the solid
view; pages up to 192 MB in the chat; and a construct a subagent built opens live in the chat.
