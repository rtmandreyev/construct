# Construct v1.3.0

Worlds: whole constructs placed together in one scene, made in Hermes from the chat. Your chair, clock and piano in
your house, your cars under it, a ship overhead, a planet in the sky; a street, a city, a game map. The full list is
in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md), and the format in [Worlds](../WORLDS.md).

## A world keeps each construct whole

A world is not a bigger construct. Each placed construct keeps its parts, sources, provenance, critic's verdicts and
joints under its placement's id (`vespa/legshield`). A world has budgets of its own, places other worlds eight levels
deep and compiles a construct placed many times once, so a city can hold districts and a district buildings.

## Placed honestly

Things stand on the part they rest on (`on: villa/living-floor-east`) at real size. A planet goes in the sky at its
real angular size: Saturn as close as the Moon is 17.8° across, its rings twice that. The world's audit says what
overlaps, what runs into the house it stands in, what stands where a door or lid opens, and what floats.

## Everywhere a construct goes

The page draws every joint of a world (they live in a texture now, not sixteen slots) and gives each view a button.
A world exports as one glTF for Blender or as a Godot game map whose doors still swing, and the Blender add-on
imports it.

## In Hermes

`construct_world` makes a world from the chat and shows each change live: place several constructs at once, move,
remove, list, name views. Constructs come from your projects, the plugin's own, or the gallery by name. Three worlds
ship to start from:
- the shed with the Samba and the Vespa;
- the Villa Savoye lived in, with fifteen constructs and Saturn;
- a street of landmarks.

Tested in Hermes Desktop with GPT-6.1 Sol and DeepSeek V4 Pro. What each had to work around became the tool's own.
