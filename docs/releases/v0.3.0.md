# Construct v0.3.0

![The Arecibo Observatory construct building its cables and platform in step with the music](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rtmandreyev/construct/main/docs/media/film-arecibo.gif)

Film mode. A construct can now assemble across a whole track of music, and you can see the music
in it: the build nearly pauses in the quiet passages and surges in the loud ones, the light
swells and dims, the construct flares on the strong attacks, and each new section of the music
starts a new stage of the construct. You get a video with the track in it. Film mode is entirely
optional: without it, Construct makes exactly what 0.2.1 made.

## What's new

- **Film mode.** `construct audio track.wav` measures a track frame by frame: its loudness, its
  brightness, its attacks and where it changes section. `construct video scene.json --assembly
  <seconds> --audio features.json --sound track.wav` renders the construct assembling across it,
  with the track in the MP4. See [Film mode](https://github.com/rtmandreyev/construct/blob/main/docs/FILM.md).
- **The music shows.** On five calm synth tracks, the build's pace over any four seconds now
  ranges from under a tenth to over three times an even build, and the light from 60% to 140%.
- **A reactivity setting.** `--reactivity` from 0 (an even build, the music only as soundtrack) to 2;
  the default, 1, makes the music clearly visible even on calm, ambient tracks.
- **Choose the camera.** `--camera tour` plays the scene's own camera flights, keeping each in its
  place as the build lengthens; `steady` is one long shot that drifts and breathes; `still` holds
  the view.
- **Agents know the workflow.** Ask *"make a construct of New Horizons that assembles across this
  track"*, and the skill authors the construct, measures the track, renders the film and reviews
  its frames. In testing, DeepSeek in Hermes did exactly that from that one sentence.
- **Five films in the gallery,** made by DeepSeek in masterpiece mode, with music by Artyom
  Andreyev, used with permission: <https://rtmandreyev.github.io/construct/#films>
- **An optional three.js loader** for filmmakers who want a construct inside their own 3-D scene.
- **Docs for people new to Construct:** how a construct is made, the Measured Light style,
  troubleshooting, and a docs index with a short glossary.

## Strictly optional

Film mode lives in its own script, which a page carries only when you build it for film
(`build --film`, or `video` with a film option). Without it, the viewer is the 0.2.1 viewer byte
for byte, and `check`, `build`, `review` and `video` write the same files as 0.2.1 did; the tests
check both. The contract stays `construct/0.2`: film mode adds to it and changes nothing in it.

## Install

```sh
git clone https://github.com/rtmandreyev/construct.git
cd construct
python3 construct_hologram/package.py --out dist
```

Then add `dist/claude-code/construct` to Claude Code (`claude --plugin-dir dist/claude-code/construct`)
or copy `dist/hermes/construct` into `~/.hermes/skills/`, and ask in plain words. See the
[README](https://github.com/rtmandreyev/construct#readme) and [USING.md](https://github.com/rtmandreyev/construct/blob/main/docs/USING.md).

## Requirements

Python 3.10 or newer, with no third-party packages. Review stills and films need a
Chromium-family browser you already have; videos and films need ffmpeg.

## Known limitations

- Film mode follows the attacks it hears, not a beat grid: there is no tempo or bar detection.
- Sections come from changes in loudness and brightness, not harmony or melody.
- A film renders at about a frame or two a second on a laptop: a four-minute film takes about an
  hour.
- The voice shimmer needs a separate vocal stem.
- Glass is never tinted, and stills show the closed pose; open a construct in a browser to see it
  open.
- No imported meshes, CAD or photogrammetry; a construct is built from Construct's own shapes.
- One periodic motion per part.
- Masterpiece mode in Hermes needs a higher helper-agent limit; see
  [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/rtmandreyev/construct/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
- Tested on macOS; review capture on Linux and Windows is untested.
