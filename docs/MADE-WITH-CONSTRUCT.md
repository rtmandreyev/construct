## Sonastra

[Sonastra](https://open.spotify.com/artist/2sZD4kxpyZt6QhkxhXZLEt) is the author's music project. Its visual identity,
for the album *Unseen/Seen* and its film, is made of more than a dozen designed constructs:
- the starship ARGUS and its lander;
- the signal's stream (*The Source*) and Object 001 (*First Contact*);
- the jellyfish and comb jelly of an ocean world (*Arrival*);
- the galaxy route and star tracker on the ship's displays.

None of these pictures was generated on its own: each is a camera, a light and a composition on a construct that
already existed. So the ship keeps the same parts, the same sizes and the same docked lander in every one, from a
Spotify gallery image to a Blender set.

### How it was made

- **The constructs** are ordinary Construct scenes, each made in masterpiece rounds with an independent critic.
- **Stills and loops** are rendered from each construct with Construct's three.js loader, choosing only the camera, the
  framing, the line weight and the light.
- **In the film,** the constructs come into Blender as Construct's glTF exports, and are then lit, finished or shown as
  holograms. The screens draw them in Measured Light.
- **When a part changes,** every picture is rebuilt from the same scene, not regenerated.

## CUDA Compatibility

[CUDA Compatibility](https://cudacompat.com) is an independent research project that runs selected CUDA 13 PyTorch
operations on older NVIDIA GPUs. Its site opens on a construct of its GM200 reference card assembling itself, from the
circuit board outward. The project is not about Construct; it used one to show the hardware it is about.
