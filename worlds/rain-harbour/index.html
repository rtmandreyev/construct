Rain Harbour, a night street

A game level from a concept picture: 60 m of a rain-soaked night street in an invented harbour district, its towers, bilingual neon signs (real English and Chinese that mean what they say) and one drivable hero car. 21 constructs placed 50 times, each made in masterpiece rounds; then the world itself ran 12 rounds against the picture. Designed, not surveyed.
