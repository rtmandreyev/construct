Construct · examples

Holographic constructs, built by AI agents

Each of these was made by an AI agent with Construct, in fast or masterpiece mode, and drawn in the Measured Light style. Open one to watch it build itself: drag to turn it around, press R to replay the assembly and L to read its labels. Some were made with earlier versions of Construct.

Films

Constructs assembling across a piece of music with film mode: the build follows the music's pace, flares on its attacks and moves on with its sections. Watch the film to see the music shape it, or open the live construct to turn it around and read its labels.
