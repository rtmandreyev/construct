Each line is one bird's track. The floor is NetLogo's world; height is time, tick 0 at the floor to tick 300 at the top.
A leaning line is a bird in flight. Near the floor the tracks cross every which way; higher up they run side by side: flocks.
The dashed rings mark alignment, how far the birds' headings agree: half at tick 49, nine in ten at tick 81, 0.98 by the end.
The floor is the 71 x 71 patch world. It wraps at its edges, so a track that leaves one side comes back on the other.
The axis at the back corner counts ticks, a mark every 60.

Each bird's track rising with time: random flight near the floor, flocks higher up. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom; R replays the assembly, which rises with the run's ticks; click it, then L hides the art direction's labels.
