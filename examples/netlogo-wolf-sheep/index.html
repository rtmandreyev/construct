The bright curve is both populations at once: sheep across the floor, wolves front to back, rising with time. Each turn of it is one cycle.
Its shadow on the floor is the classic phase plot, the same loop seen from above.
One wall carries the number of sheep against time, the other (in violet) the wolves: the two waves, a little out of step.
The markers show the first sheep peak (tick 80) and the wolves' peak 61 ticks later: predators follow their prey.
The axis counts ticks, 0 to 1,500, a mark every 300. Counts are smoothed over 25 ticks.

The predator-prey cycle rising in time: sheep across, wolves front to back, ticks up. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom; R replays the assembly, which rises with the run's ticks; click it, then L hides the art direction's labels.
