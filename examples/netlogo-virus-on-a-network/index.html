The floor is the network as NetLogo laid it out: 150 people and their links.
Each upright line rises to the tick its person was first infected; one left on the floor never was.
A climbing link joins two people infected far apart in time.
The dashed frame is the peak, found from the counts: 118 infected at once, at tick 68.

A spread on a network, in one command: each person rises to the tick they were infected; the peak marked by itself. Drag to orbit; click it, then scroll to zoom; R replays the assembly, which rises with the run's ticks.
